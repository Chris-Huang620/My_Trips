Cypress Tree Tunnel

A quarter-mile corridor of Monterey cypress trees planted in the 1930s, leading to a former transatlantic radio receiving station. The image you've seen on every Point Reyes Pinterest board. Chimney Rock
The Hike

Chimney Rock Trail

A 1.8-mile out-and-back along an open coastal headland. Wildflowers linger into early June — sun cups, paintbrush, blue-eyed grass. Elephant seals are still on the beaches if you're lucky. Exposed and often windy, so layers and a windbreaker are non-negotiable. No dogs allowed — Mochi stays in the car at the (shaded, breezy) trailhead pullout, or one person waits with him.

Point Reyes Lighthouse
The View

Point Reyes Lighthouse

Built 1870, perched on the windiest headland on the Pacific coast. The lighthouse itself sits 300 feet below the visitor center — 308 steps down, and 308 back up. The visitor center closes at 4 PM (Thu–Mon, closed Tue/Wed) but the overlook and stairs stay accessible until dusk in calm winds. Stairs close automatically when wind tops 40 mph — check the NPS conditions page that morning.

⌖ Sleeper Detail · The Hidden Hours
The light at Cypress Tunnel and the wildflowers at Chimney Rock are not the headliners — the headliners are the moments you stop the car between them. The Tomales Bay roadside pullouts north of Marshall are some of the most beautiful in California and require zero effort. Don't skip them.

Section Three · Packing

Bring this. Layers, mostly.

Point Reyes in June runs 67°F high, 53°F low. Foggy mornings, windy afternoons. Coastal headlands are typically 15–20°F cooler than the town. Star (★) items are non-negotiable.

For the people
  • Windproof shell jacket
  • Fleece or mid-layer (one each)
  • Long-sleeve base layers
  • One warm-weather outfit (in case)
  • Comfortable hiking shoes / sneakers

  • Wool socks (Chimney Rock wind)
  • Beanie or warm hat

Section Four · Notes & Reminders

Things to know before we go.

⚠ Dogs on Trails · The Big One
Most Point Reyes National Seashore trails do not allow dogs — including the Lighthouse and Chimney Rock. The only stops on this itinerary where Mochi joins are the Cypress Tree Tunnel (leashed), Heidrun (outdoor garden), Hog Island (outdoor), and the town of Point Reyes Station. For the Lighthouse and Chimney Rock, plan to take turns or sit with Mochi at the (cool, breezy, shaded) parking pullouts.
⌖ Richard's "Lobby" · The Working Patio
The Point Reyes Station Inn doesn't have a true lobby with a coffee service to camp out in early. Each room has its own Keurig. The Sun Room's private garden patio is the de facto lobby — quiet, outdoors, Wi-Fi reachable. Plan around it: pre-load a thermos, bring a portable light, and confirm the Wi-Fi password at check-in Friday evening.
⚠ Reservations to Make
Hog Island picnic tables (Saturday) — Fri–Mon they're reservation-only via Tock or SeatMe. Book before the trip if you want the grill table experience. Walk-up oysters at the Boat Bar otherwise.
Heidrun Saturday Tour ($45) — optional but books out. Reserve on exploretock.com if Chris wants it.
✓ The Morning of, Check
nps.gov/pore/planyourvisit/conditions.htm — Lighthouse stair closures (40 mph winds), any road or trail closures. Coastal fog forecast on weather.gov. If the lighthouse stairs are closed, flip Saturday's afternoon and do Chimney Rock + Tomales Point overlook instead.
